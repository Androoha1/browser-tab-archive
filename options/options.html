Browser Tab Archive

Welcome

Press ⌘. on any tab to archive it, or right-click a tab and choose Archive tab. Your archive lives in the sidebar.

Uninstalling deletes the archive. Backups in Downloads/browser-tab-archive/ stay on your computer and can be imported again.

Archiving

Ask for a note each time you archive. Unnamed groups always ask for a name.

Backups

Saved to Downloads/browser-tab-archive/ a few seconds after every change. The last 5 versions are kept.

Bookmark mirror

Keeps a copy of the archive in your bookmarks, so it survives uninstalling. Groups become subfolders.

Single tabs

Your data

Export saves the whole archive as one JSON file. Import merges a file in and skips entries you already have.
